
# Insurance Sales & Customer Insights Dashboard – Power BI Project
This Power BI dashboard was developed as part of the Codebasics Virtual Internship to analyze and visualize key metrics for Shield Insurance. The project delivers actionable insights into revenue growth, customer demographics, claims behavior, and sales performance.

# Project Overview
The dashboard empowers business stakeholders to monitor performance trends and make data-driven decisions by offering:

📈 Revenue & Customer Growth Trends

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Customer Segmentation by Age Group & City

💼 Sales Channel Performance

📍 Regional Insights

🧾 Claims & Settlements by Age Group

# Technical Highlights
Data Source: Excel-based raw data

Data Modeling: Relationships optimized for performance

DAX Measures: MoM growth, trend lines, and KPI metrics

Visuals Used: KPI Cards, Pie Charts, Line & Area Graphs

Interactivity: Dynamic filters by city, age group, and sales mode

# Key Insights
Total Revenue: ₹989.25M with 18.40% MoM growth

Total Customers: 27K with 18.28% MoM growth

31–40 age group leads revenue generation at ₹356.03M

Delhi NCR tops the charts with ₹401.57M in revenue and 11K customers

Claims highest in 65+ age group (74.36%)

Offline Agents remain the top sales channel (₹550.76M)

# Impact
This dashboard supports decision-making by identifying high-performing regions and age segments, optimizing sales channel strategies, and enhancing customer targeting efforts.
